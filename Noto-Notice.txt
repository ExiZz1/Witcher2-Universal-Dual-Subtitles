ExiZz P2 OFL fallback glyph catalog
==================================

donor.json contains a subset of outlines from the following SIL Open Font
License 1.1 fonts, converted to DefineFont3 coordinates for this experiment.

Noto Sans CJK JP
Copyright 2014–2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/), with Reserved Font Name 'Source'.
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
License: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/blob/main/Sans/LICENSE

Noto Sans
Copyright 2015–2021 Google LLC. All Rights Reserved.
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic
License notice extracted from name IDs 13 and 14 of the licensed local TTF.

The accompanying OFL-1.1.txt applies to the donor outlines.
The catalog contains no stock game glyphs. Stock SWF files, patched stock SWFs,
localization files and font binaries are not packaged.
